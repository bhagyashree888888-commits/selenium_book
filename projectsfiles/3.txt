Project Title: INTERNAL PROJECT
Employee Management Portal
Q1.TELL ME ABOUT YOUR PROJECT?
An Employee Management Portal (EMP) is a web-based or software 
application designed to streamline and centralize employee-related 
processes within an organization. It helps HR teams, managers, and 
employees handle tasks such as ESS, leave, performance reviews etc.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q2.KEY FEATURES?
Key Features of an Employee Management Portal:
Employee Database – Stores employee details, including personal information, job roles, and work history.
Attendance & Leave Management – Tracks working hours, attendance, and leave requests.
Payroll Management – Manages salary processing, tax deductions, and payslip generation.
Performance Management – Includes goal setting, performance evaluations, and feedback mechanisms.
Employee Self-Service (ESS) – Allows employees to access payslips, request leaves, and update personal information.
Recruitment & Onboarding – Helps with job postings, candidate tracking, and onboarding new hires.
Training & Development – Provides training resources, courses, and skill development programs.
Task & Project Management – Assigns and monitors tasks or projects assigned to employees.
Document Management – Stores and organizes company policies, contracts, and other HR documents.
Communication & Notifications – Enables internal messaging, announcements, and alerts.
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q3.DIFFER MODULES IN EMP?
1.Employee Self-Service (ESS) Module:
Employees can update their personal information.
Access to payslips, leave requests, and work schedules.
Internal messaging system for communication.
                            --------------------------------------------
2.Document Management Module
Centralized storage for HR policies, company handbooks, and employee-related documents.
Secure access and permission controls.
                 -------------------------------------------------------
3.Training & Development Module
Online learning, training courses, and skill development.
Certification tracking and progress monitoring.
Employee feedback on training programs.
               -------------------------------------------------------
4.Employee Information Management Module
Stores employee profiles, contact details, job roles, and work history.
Includes document uploads (ID proofs, certificates, etc.).
               -----------------------------------------------------
5.Attendance & Leave Management Module
Tracks employee attendance, clock-in/out times, and work hours.
Leave application and approval system.
Holiday calendar and leave balance tracking.
            ------------------------------------------------------------
6.Task & Project Management Module
Assigning tasks to employees.
Tracking project deadlines and progress.
Collaboration tools for teams.
              ----------------------------------------------------
7. Communication & Notification Module
Internal announcements and alerts.
Email and push notifications for approvals, reminders, and updates.
Employee feedback and survey tools.
